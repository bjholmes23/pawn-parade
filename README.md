# Pawn Parade

A family-friendly cross-and-circle race game in the Pachisi family. Plain HTML/CSS/JS with no build step and no external dependencies.

Play it at https://parchee.si

- `?demo=1` – all four seats are computer players.
- `?speed=0.5` – scale animation/AI delays.

## Rules
- 2 dice. A pawn leaves its nest on a 5 (one die, or both dice totalling 5).
- Use each die on a pawn, or the total on one pawn. If you can move, you must.
- Doubles: roll again. Third doubles in a row sends your furthest pawn home to the nest.
- Land on a lone enemy pawn (not on a star) to bump it: +20 bonus. Get a pawn home: +10 bonus.
- Two pawns of one color make a wall nobody may pass. First to get all 4 home wins.
